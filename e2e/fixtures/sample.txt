Introduction to Neural Networks

Neural networks are computational models inspired by the human brain. They consist of layers of interconnected nodes, or neurons, that process information using connectionist approaches.

Architecture

A typical neural network has three types of layers:
1. Input layer - receives raw data
2. Hidden layers - transform the data through weighted connections
3. Output layer - produces the final prediction or classification

Training

Neural networks learn through a process called backpropagation. During training, the network makes predictions, calculates the error against known labels, and adjusts weights to minimize that error using gradient descent.

Applications

Neural networks are used in image recognition, natural language processing, speech recognition, and many other fields. Deep learning, which uses many hidden layers, has revolutionized AI research since 2012.
